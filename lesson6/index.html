What independence means

In probability

For categorical variables, independence requires the joint probability of every pair to equal the product of its marginal probabilities. Equivalently, P(F = f | X = x) = P(F = f) for each x with positive probability and every f.

In statistics

Sample counts fluctuate even when the population is independent. We test H₀: independence against H₁: association. The p-value is the upper-tail probability under H₀, using the chi-square approximation. A large p-value does not prove independence.

Association and causality

Differences in login outcomes may reflect user experience, device type or account policy. This table alone cannot establish that changing the login method causes a change in failure rates. Repeated attempts by the same user may also violate the independence of observations assumed by the test.
