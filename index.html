How to read this notebook

Each assignment combines an explanation, a live experiment and a discussion of its limits.

01 / Follow the computation

Inputs and intermediate results stay visible. The toy arithmetic is implemented directly; the wallet lab uses versioned cryptographic libraries.

02 / Inspect the measurements

Charts are calculated from the experiment. Tables and CSV exports make the underlying observations available for inspection.

03 / Separate evidence from claims

A distribution can reveal a pattern or a bias. Passing a small statistical test does not prove cryptographic security.
