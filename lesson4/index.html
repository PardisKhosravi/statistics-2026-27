The connection

Welford changes how the statistics are computed; sampling theory explains how those statistics behave across repeated samples.

One stream, constant memory

Each new score updates a count, a mean and SS. A complete history is not needed to calculate the running estimates.

Many streams, a sampling distribution

Each hacker contributes one mean and one sample variance. Their averages estimate μ and σ²; their spread measures the precision of a single hacker’s estimates.
